Frequently Asked Questions

What is OTP Relay?

OTP Relay is a secure service designed to manage and distribute One-Time Passwords for government operations.

Does the officer still receive the OTP?

Yes, the OTP is delivered to the officer's device as usual; OTP Relay securely routes the authorized OTP to the designated operator according to the office's routing rules.

Can staff choose which sender IDs are processed?

Yes. Staff explicitly authorize the government sender IDs that OTP Relay is allowed to process. Unrelated SMS messages are ignored.

Can operators see OTPs from all staff?

No. Access is restricted based on the office hierarchy and specific routing rules configured by the administrator.

What happens if internet connectivity is unavailable?

If internet connectivity is temporarily unavailable, the authorized Android app can securely queue the OTP event and synchronize it when connectivity is restored. SMS/mobile-network availability is separate from internet connectivity.

What happens if a device is lost?

Administrators can revoke a device from the central dashboard, preventing further OTP submissions once the device synchronizes.

Can Office Admin configure OTP routing?

Administrators can configure routing rules to send OTPs from specific senders or services to designated operators.

Does OTP Relay bypass government authentication?

No. OTP Relay does not replace or bypass a government department's authentication system. It only securely relays authorized OTP messages internally after the staff member has explicitly enabled the service.
