📱 OTP Relay Installation Guide

Follow these steps to install the app on your phone

⚠️ Important: Play Protect Warning

Google Play Protect may block the installation. This is NORMAL — the app reads SMS for OTP extraction. Follow the steps below to install.

1 Download the APK File
Download the OTP Relay APK file from your admin or team leader.
The file will be named like: otp-relay-v1.2.0.apk
2 Open the APK File
Tap on the downloaded APK file to start installation.
3 Play Protect Warning — DO NOT TAP BLOCK!
You will see a warning: "App blocked to protect your device"
👆 Tap "More details" (NOT "Block")
The "Block" button is highlighted, but you need to tap the small "More details" link below it.
⬇️ You will see something like this ⬇️

⚠️ App blocked
This app can request access to sensitive data...

▶ More details
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Block
4 Tap "Install anyway"
After tapping "More details", you will see a new option.
👆 Tap "Install anyway"
This will install the app despite the Play Protect warning.
⬇️ You will see ⬇️

More details

OTP Relay can access: SMS messages...

▶ Install anyway
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Don't install
5 App Installed!
The app will install normally. Open it and follow the setup steps.
✅ Done! The app is now installed and ready to use.
💡 Alternative: Disable Play Protect (One-time)
If you don't want to see the warning every time:

1. Open Play Store app
2. Tap your profile icon (top right)
3. Tap Play Protect
4. Tap Settings (gear icon)
5. Turn OFF "Scan apps with Play Protect"

You can turn it back on after installation.
